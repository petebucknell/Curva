Curva

Everything on this page is a proposal. Change any part and we'll update the site to match.

What Curva is

Curva helps a group follow one planned road trip, stop by stop. Curva is the Italian and Portuguese word for "curve", which suits the winding roads it's built for.

Logo

A road curving through a rounded square. It works in one colour too.

Colours

Road blue#2b6cb0. Main buttons, progress, and the logo.
Ink#1a1a1a. Main text.
Slate#6b7280. Secondary text and labels.
Mist#c9ced6. Borders and dividers.
Stop red#b42318. Errors only, never decoration.

Type

Headline, 26px bold

Section heading, 18px

Body text, 16px. Clear and short sentences.

Label, 12px capitals

Font: the system font on each phone (San Francisco on iPhone, Roboto on Android). No extra downloads, so the site loads fast.

Voice

  • Clear. "Mark as reached", not "Check in!"
  • Calm. Short sentences. No exclamation marks.
  • Practical. Say what happens next: "Everyone sees this update."

Open decisions

  • Do you like the road-blue colour, or would you like something different?
  • Do you want a tagline under the name?
  • Should the logo include the word "Curva", or stay as the symbol alone?
